Critique of v4

Your list, checked

One-off sizes, four radii, per-page spacing. Real.
Ten text sizes are in use (10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 30). v5 has five sizes, one radius and three spacing steps for grouping.
Page headers drifted. Real.
Titles run 20 to 30px across three container widths. v5: one 28px Sora title, no subtitle, an optional action on the right, one 720px column.
Uppercase 10px label doing five jobs. Real, and the loudest AI tell in the app.
It is the eyebrow recipe, and at 10px in neutral-500 it also fails contrast. v5 drops tracked caps. Zone names become 12px sentence case; control labels stay uppercase only because users type them that way for the device.
Three button looks plus one-offs. Real, and primary is not primary.
The primary button is a 10% accent tint with an outline, so it carries less weight than a heading. v5: filled primary, bordered secondary, text-only quiet. Destructive is a color modifier, not a fourth look.
Inconsistent icons. Real.
The same app icon also repeats on all four tiles, which tells the reader nothing. v5: text-only navigation, real app icons at 16, 20 and 32px, one stroke set (16px, 1.5 stroke) for the few actions that need one.
Accent carries too many meanings. Real.
Active nav, selection, links and suggestions move to neutral ink. Accent keeps three jobs (see Rules).
Glass versus solid is easy to break. Partly.
The material rule is fine; the names are wrong. One glass surface survives: the Notice window, which really sits over the desktop. The device card and hero card become solid.
No empty, loading or error pattern. Real.
v5 has one state block with three variants and one line of copy each.
Website drifted by 10 tokens. Real.
Both apps import one tokens.css, so drift becomes a merge conflict instead of a silent difference.

What the list missed

Contrast fails where text is smallest.
neutral-500 is 4.15:1 and neutral-600 is 3.4:1 on the card fill, and those carry the 10 to 12px captions. White on the accent fill is 3.66:1. Input borders are about 1.3:1 against the 3:1 non-text rule.
Shadows nobody can see.
Five black shadow recipes on a #08090a canvas. On near-black a shadow reads as dirt or as nothing. v5 has no drop shadows: height is a fill step plus a hairline.
v3 residue that still reads as generated.
16px radius on everything, the top-lit gradient on keycaps, hover lift, staggered rise-in on lists, scale-in modals, the accent-tinted nav pill with a tinted border, and #4c7eff, a near neighbour of the framework blue.
Motion has seven curves and runs to 420ms.
Two keyframes are identical (settle and node-in). v5: three durations, none over 220ms, two curves.
Token names lie.
neutral-800 to 950 are surfaces, not text. GLASS_PANEL is solid. violet is dead. v5 names tokens for their role (ink-1, line-strong), so the name is the rule.
Success, error and gold are three unrelated hues.
Success needs no color; a check and the word do it. v5 keeps one error color and the hardware gold.
Decisions live in comments.
surfaces.ts is mostly history. The why belongs in DESIGN.md; the code should be short enough to read.

Type: what stays

Sora stays for titles and headings, where its rounded geometry echoes the wordmark. It is held to 16px and up at 600, because its width wastes room below that. JetBrains Mono stays for shortcut text only (Ctrl+F5), never labels or chrome. Inter goes: it is the default for generated interfaces and gives the 12 to 14px UI no voice. The directions differ in the face that replaces it.

Also worth keeping

Solid surfaces, one accent, real application icons, the global focus-visible rule, the reduced-motion handling, dark as the theme, and the 12-character label limit tied to the device display.

Both use Sora, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and JetBrains Mono, on dark surfaces with one accent. They differ in material, accent and radius. Select one; the rest of the page follows.

Radius and elevation

One radius: {{ d.r }} on every button, field, card, tile and modal. Full only for the toggle and the device dot. Nested shapes use the radius minus the gap (the segmented control).

Three levels, no drop shadows. 0 canvas. 1 card: card fill and a hairline. 2 overlay: raised fill, strong hairline, 60% black scrim behind. Keycaps add one inset bottom edge, the skirt of a real key.

Icons

Real app icons at 16 (inline), 20 (rows, cards) and 32 (modal headers, onboarding). UI icons from one set, Lucide at 16px and 1.5 stroke, only for close, chevrons, check and drag. No icon in navigation.

Motion

100msColor and press feedback. Press moves 1px down. 160msEnter: modal, toast, menu, page. Fade plus 4px rise, ease-out. Exit is a 100ms fade, ease-in. 220msLayout: collapse, toggle travel, zone highlight on glide.

Curves: ease-out cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), ease-in cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1). No stagger, lift, scale or pop. Reduced motion keeps fades, drops every translate.

Rules

What accent may mean

Accent means you can act on this, now. Three uses only: the one primary button, the focus ring, and the on-state of a toggle or checkbox.

It never marks where you are (nav, selection), what Noma suggests, a link, a count or success. Those use ink, weight and words. A Flow suggestion is signalled by its heading ("Noticed today") and its real count.

Do

  • Use the five sizes and seven roles. A new size means changing the system, not the page.
  • Lay out four zones as 2 by 2 in trackpad order, never a row of four.
  • Write control labels the device can show: 12 characters, the user's own words.
  • Give each view one primary button.
  • Say what Noma noticed with the real number: "11 times today".

Don't

  • Uppercase or letter-space anything except a control label.
  • Add drop shadows, gradients, or blur outside the Notice window.
  • Animate hover, stagger lists, or scale anything in.
  • Mute text with opacity. Pick an ink.
  • Put a subtitle under a page title, or text glyphs where an icon belongs.

Light theme

Not in v5. Dark is a decision here: Noma sits beside editors and players that are mostly dark, and the device display is light-on-black. A light theme would cost 16 more values, re-measuring every pair in the table, a second hardware color, and double the screenshot QA. Because every color is a CSS variable, it can arrive later as one [data-theme] block with no component changes.

Recommendation: A, Machined

Ship Machined. It keeps what v4 got right (graphite, one blue, solid surfaces, Sora and JetBrains Mono) and fixes what failed: the cobalt clears 7:1 as a fill under dark text where #4c7eff failed under white, every caption clears 4.5:1, and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next keeps 1, l and I distinct in the 12px captions where most of this app lives.

Display is the stronger identity but sits closest to the near-black terminal template, and a white primary competes with Sora headings. Borrow its idea that the app speaks the device's language: labels as typed, 12 characters.
